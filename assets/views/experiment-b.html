How far the reading sits from the true air temperature

Course model
— The thermocouple does not read the air. It sits between the hot wall and the cool air, so it settles above the air temperature — — instead of the true value.

Why: radiation in balances convection out

At steady state the two flows are equal by definition, so their sizes say nothing. What sets the error is the ratio of the two coefficients.

T6 · grey ε 0.4 · 0.5 mm T7 · black ε 0.95 · 0.5 mm T8 · black ε 0.95 · 3 mm

The line colour is the bead surface and the line thickness is its diameter, so T7 and T8 share a colour and differ only in weight. Raising the velocity raises the convection coefficient, which pulls the bead toward the air temperature. A larger bead has a lower coefficient for the same flow, so T8 stays hottest. T6 gains less radiation to begin with because its emissivity is lower.

What this assumed

read before trusting the numbers

Conduction along the thermocouple wire is not modelled. Real wires drain heat away from the bead, so the true error is smaller than what is shown here.

Bead diameters, emissivities and the view factor are placeholder values. The T6 emissivity is yours to set because the course notes do not give it.
